Need help with Loom? Email **loomcurationapp@gmail.com** and we'll get back to you.

## Common questions

**How do I report content or someone's behavior?**
Tap **Report** on any rating, review, comment, or profile. We review reports and aim to act on
objectionable content within 24 hours. See our
[Community Guidelines]({{ site.baseurl }}/guidelines).

**How do I block someone?**
Open their profile and choose **Block**. Blocked accounts can't interact with you or see your
activity.

**How do I delete my account?**
Go to **Settings → Delete Account**. This permanently removes your profile and the content
associated with it, including photos you uploaded. See the
[Privacy Policy]({{ site.baseurl }}/privacy) for details on what's retained.

**How do I turn notifications off?**
Notification permissions are controlled in the iOS **Settings** app, under Loom.

**How do I make a review private?**
Mark the review private when you post or edit it. Private reviews aren't shown to other users.

## Reporting a safety concern

For anything urgent involving safety, email **loomcurationapp@gmail.com** with "Safety" in the
subject line.
