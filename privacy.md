## Overview

Loom ("Loom", "we", "us") is a social app for rating and discovering beauty products and seeing
what your friends use. This policy explains what information Loom collects, how it is used, and the
choices you have. By creating an account or using Loom, you agree to this policy.

## Information We Collect

**Account & identity.** When you sign up with Sign in with Apple or Google, we receive your name and
email address to create and secure your account. If you use Sign in with Apple's "Hide My Email,"
we receive the relayed address only.

**Profile & content you create.** Your username, display name, bio, avatar, and the content you post
— product ratings, rankings, reviews, comments, collections, and follows. Much of this is **public
or visible to other users** by design (for example, your ratings and reviews appear in feeds and on
your profile). Reviews you mark private are not shown to others.

**Photos.** Photos you add to a review are uploaded to our cloud storage and shown alongside that
review. Granting camera or photo-library access is only used to let you capture or select these
photos; we do not access your library otherwise.

**Device & technical data.** If you enable notifications, we store a push notification token for your
device so we can deliver alerts. We also process basic technical data needed to operate the service.

## How We Use Information

We use your information to operate Loom: to authenticate you, display and share the content you post,
power social features (feeds, follows, notifications), enforce our Terms and Community Guidelines, and
respond to support and safety reports.

## How Information Is Stored and Shared

Loom's backend is hosted on **Supabase**, our cloud infrastructure provider, which stores and
processes your data on our behalf as a sub-processor. We do **not** sell your personal information,
and we do **not** use third-party advertising or cross-app tracking. Public content you post is
visible to other Loom users and may be seen by anyone using the app.

## Data Retention and Account Deletion

You can permanently delete your account at any time from **Settings → Delete Account**. Deleting your
account removes your profile and the content associated with it from our systems, including your
uploaded photos. Some content you contributed to shared catalog data may be retained in anonymized
form (with your identity removed). We may retain limited records where required for legal or safety
reasons.

## Children's Privacy

Loom is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your region), and we do not
knowingly collect their information.

## Your Choices

You can edit your profile, control notification permissions in iOS Settings, mark reviews private,
block other users, and delete your account. Depending on where you live, you may have additional
rights over your personal data; contact us to exercise them.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected here with a new date.
